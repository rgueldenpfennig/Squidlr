Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines the types of personal information collected and received by squidlr.com ("the Website") and how that information is used. By accessing or using the Website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

The Website is a static, purely informational website. It no longer offers a video download service and does not process any links or content from social media platforms.

Information Collection and Use

Hosting and Log Files

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the Website, GitHub may automatically collect technical information such as your internet protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system, referring page and date/time of the request. This data is processed by GitHub to deliver the Website and to ensure its security and stability. The operator of the Website has no access to these log files. For more information, please refer to the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Cookies and Local Storage

The Website does not use any cookies, tracking technologies or analytics services.

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is stored solely in the local storage of your browser so that it is remembered on your next visit. This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Personal Information

The Website does not collect any additional personal information.

Third-Party Links

The Website may contain links to third-party websites or services that are not owned or controlled by us. We have no control over, and assume no responsibility for, the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those third parties before providing any personal information.

Children's Privacy

The Website is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under the age of 13, please contact us, and we will take immediate steps to remove the information from our systems.

Updates to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting the revised policy on the Website. Your continued use of the Website after any such changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@squidlr.com.
