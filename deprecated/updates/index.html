Updates and history of Squidlr

  • November 9, 2026

    Squidlr has been discontinued

    After more than three years, the Squidlr online video downloader has been shut down. My family and my work need my time now, and keeping a web-based download service working while social media platforms constantly change is a job of its own.

    If you still want to save videos, have a look at yt-dlp for desktop and Seal for Android. The source code stays available on GitHub.

    Thank you for using Squidlr!

  • January 16, 2025

    New Feature: Facebook Video Download

    From now on Squidlr also supports the download of videos from Facebook.

  • September 22, 2024

    New Feature: LinkedIn Video Download

    From now on Squidlr also supports the download of videos from LinkedIn.

  • July 22, 2024

    New Feature: TikTok Video Download

    From now on Squidlr enables you to download videos from TikTok. With this new feature, you can easily save and enjoy your favorite TikTok videos offline.

  • June 30, 2024

    Squidlr is now fully open source!

    If you are interested to see how Squidlr works under the hood, or you want to get involved in the development process, then feel free to visit GitHub.

  • November 5, 2023

    New Feature: Instagram Video Download

    I'm happy to announce that Squidlr now allows you to download videos from Instagram. With this new feature, you can easily save and enjoy your favorite Instagram videos offline.

  • October 10, 2023

    Support my work with Ko-Fi

    Now you can support my work by making a donation through Ko-Fi. Your contribution helps to sustain the hosting and further development of Squidlr. Visit my Ko-Fi page to show your support.

    Thank you for being a part of my journey.

  • July 29, 2023

    Announcing Squidlr: Download Twitter Videos

    I'm quite excited to finally introduce Squidlr, your go-to solution for downloading Twitter videos.

    With Squidlr, you can effortlessly save and share any Twitter video, anytime, anywhere.

    I also wrote about the initial release and thoughts on my blog. Sorry, only available in German though ;)
